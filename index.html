A note from us

Commercial real estate is a relationship business. The work around it shouldn't take the relationship away.

01 — Productive

Reclaim the hours buried in the back office.

Brokers shouldn't be the most expensive data-entry clerks in commercial real estate. CREOPS automates the busywork around the deal so the day belongs to deals.

  • i.

    Pipeline that updates itself

    CRM hygiene, deal stages, and follow-ups synced from email, calendar, and call notes — without broker hand-feeding.

  • ii.

    Tour reports in minutes, not days

    Site visits become client-ready summaries with photos, callouts, and follow-ups drafted before you're back at the car.

  • iii.

    Comps, BOVs, and OMs on demand

    Market comps, BOVs, and proposal decks assembled in your firm's brand voice — in a single prompt.

02 — Transformative

Not a chatbot. An operating model.

CREOPS sits underneath the brokerage — connecting CRM, documents, market data, and inbox into one fabric. The result isn't faster busywork. It's a different way to run the firm.

  • i.

    One source of truth

    Deal data, client preferences, and market context, queryable from chat, dashboard, or directly inside your CRM.

  • ii.

    Signals, not noise

    Lease expirations, tenant moves, ownership changes — surfaced as actionable signals routed to the right broker, the right day.

  • iii.

    A firm that compounds

    Every deal trains the firm's collective intuition. Top-producer judgement becomes institutional, not personal.

03 — Ethical

AI you can put in front of a client.

Commercial real estate runs on fiduciary trust. CREOPS is built so brokers stay in command — transparent reasoning, defensible sources, and your data kept inside your walls.

  • i.

    Your data stays yours

    Single-tenant deployments, BYO-LLM, SSO, and full audit logs. Your deals never train someone else's model.

  • ii.

    Sourced, every time

    Every figure and recommendation links back to the document, comp, or message it came from. Verifiable by default.

  • iii.

    Broker-in-the-loop

    The system drafts; the broker decides. Approval gates and reviewer trails on every client-facing output.

AI shouldn't replace the broker. It should set them free — to do the part of this work that only a human can.
